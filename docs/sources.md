# Sources by market

Link priority for every results document: **company website first, exchange or regulator copy second.**

| Market | Index | Events | Primary source | Fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US | S&P 500 | 8-K 2.02 results, 8-K 5.07 meeting votes, 10-Q, 10-K, DEF 14A | Company IR press release | SEC EDGAR filing index |
| UK | FTSE 100 | Results, trading statements, AGMs, capital markets days, annual reports | Company IR archive | London Stock Exchange RNS (a few sites refuse automated reads) |
| Japan | Nikkei 225 | Kessan Tanshin with release time, AGM notices, briefings | Company IR page | TSE TDnet (IRBANK archive) |
| South Korea | KOSPI 200 | Provisional results, IR notices, AGM results, periodic reports | Company IR page | DART (FSS) |
| Australia | S&P/ASX 200 | Appendix 4D/4E, quarterlies, AGMs, investor days, annual reports | Company IR page | ASX announcements |
| India | Nifty 50, Sensex | Results, earnings calls, analyst meets, annual reports | Company website | BSE / NSE filings (via Screener document lists) |
| All | — | IPOs, DRHP / S-1 / prospectuses | — | SEC, SEBI, NSE, BSE SME, JPX, KRX, ASX, LSE |
